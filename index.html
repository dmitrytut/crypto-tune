About

Crypto Tune turns a live crypto futures market into calm generative music. Leave it in a background tab and hear what the market is doing without watching a chart.

A quiet market is a soft drone with a slow melody and bells. When the price moves sharply, the tempo rises, the filters open and a bass comes in. A rising price lifts the melody, a falling one pulls it down. Liquidations sound as gongs, large trades as plucks.

Buys rise as bubbles, sells fall as drops. The bigger the trade, the bigger the mark:

Tap or drag through the particles. Turn on Interaction sounds in Settings for quiet accents while Listen is on. On short or zoomed-in screens, vertical swipes scroll the page instead; you can still tap or swipe across.

Plays any futures pair, or a simulated market to hear every sound on demand. Free, in the browser, no sign-up.
